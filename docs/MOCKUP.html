How to read this report

Usual load is the median reading for the same feeder, hour and day type (weekday, Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) over the 8 weeks before.

An alert is raised when a reading is 5 typical spreads above or below the usual load, or when a meter repeats the same value for 6 hours. Alerts arrive with each hourly batch.

Explained by matches an alert to the switching log (planned load transfers) or to an event the generator planted. Alerts matching neither are noise or real surprises.

Forecast is made at 23:00 for the next day, from the last day and the last 4 weeks, the hour, day type, public holidays and temperature. Error is the absolute miss divided by actual demand (WAPE), per day.

Same hour last week is the simple forecast the model has to beat. The gain is shown only when its 95% range is above zero.

Planted events caught counts only events that ended 24 hours or more before the data cut-off.

About the data

Synthetic. The generator draws hourly load from daily and weekly shapes by feeder type, temperature, growth and the 2026 public holidays for Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, then plants spikes, drops, stuck meters and two slow drifts, and logs planned load transfers. Built on Microsoft Fabric: Eventhouse, Lakehouse, Fabric ML, Direct Lake.
